Start by breaking the triangle of balls. After the break, answer a question to unlock each turn's shot. Drag BACK from the white cue ball and release: the farther you drag, the harder you hit. Balls collide, bounce off cushions, slow down, and only score if they actually fall in a pocket. A miss earns no points. Each player gets one shot per turn. If the cue ball goes in, it is a scratch and is reset for the next player. The 8-ball is worth one point like the others.
